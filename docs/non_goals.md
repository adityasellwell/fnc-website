# Non-Goals (Version 1)

The following features and integrations are explicitly **out of scope** for the current version (F&C Fresh Version 1). Any requests or proposals for these items will be deferred to future milestones.

- ❌ **Loyalty Program**: Point accumulation, tiering, or reward redemption.
- ❌ **Referral Program**: Invite codes, share links, or referee discounts.
- ❌ **Subscription Orders**: Recurring deliveries, calendar-based scheduling, or subscription billing.
- ❌ **Warehouse Management**: Bin tracking, pick list generation, multi-stage sorting, or supplier receiving workflows.
- ❌ **Franchise Portal**: Dedicated dashboards or reporting tools for franchise store owners.
- ❌ **Supplier Portal**: Supplier invoices, purchase ordering, or direct supplier stocking.
- ❌ **AI Recommendations**: Machine learning recommendation grids, personalized dynamic sorting, or automated search adjustments.
- ❌ **Live Rider Tracking**: Real-time GPS tracking on maps, active rider apps, or automated third-party logistics integrations (e.g., Dunzo, Shadowfax).
